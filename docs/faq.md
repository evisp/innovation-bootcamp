## About the bootcamp { #about }

??? question "What is Innovation Bootcamp Albania?"

    A five-day programme where students, mentors and companies work together on real industry challenges. Companies bring problems they are facing right now, student teams explore and validate them, build a first solution and pitch it on the last day. The idea is to bring universities and businesses closer, and to give students practice in starting from a real need rather than from a technology.

??? question "Who organises it?"

    The bootcamp is organised by [Match4Research](partners.md) and the Faculty of Economy at the University of Tirana, with support from universities, international partners and local institutions in Durrës. You can see all of them on the [Partners](partners.md) page.

??? question "When and where does it take place?"

    From Monday 26 to Friday 30 October 2026, at Godina Liria and in Durrës.

## Before you arrive { #before }

??? question "Do I need to prepare anything?"

    Yes. Go through the [training modules](training/index.md) before the day each one is used. They are short videos and tools, so the time on site goes into hands-on work with your team instead of lectures. Module 4 is optional.

??? question "What should I bring?"

    - A laptop and charger
    - Comfortable shoes for the evening walk along the Adriatic
    - Something to note ideas on, paper or digital
    - An outfit for White Sensation Night on Friday

    <span class="ib-tbc">To be confirmed</span> Anything else the organisers want you to bring.

## During the week { #during }

??? question "What does a normal day look like?"

    Sessions run from 09:00 to 16:15, with a short break in the morning, lunch at 12:15 and another break in the afternoon. Dinner is at 19:00 and there is something planned every evening at 20:00. The [agenda](agenda.md) has the full programme.

??? question "How does mentoring work?"

    Mentors rotate between teams during work sessions, so you can ask for help whenever you are stuck. On Wednesday and Thursday mornings each team gives a short progress pitch and gets feedback, and on Friday morning there are pitch clinics to polish your final presentation. The [Mentors](mentors.md) page shows who to ask for what.

??? question "Do I have to join the evening events?"

    <span class="ib-tbc">To be confirmed</span> They are not formal sessions, but they are a big part of the week. It is where you get to know the mentors, the companies and the other teams, so we strongly encourage coming.

??? question "Can I switch teams?"

    <span class="ib-tbc">To be confirmed</span> Teams settle on Monday afternoon. After that, changes are only possible in exceptional cases, in agreement with the mentors.

## Demo day { #demo-day }

??? question "What happens on Friday?"

    Pitch clinics with mentors in the morning, final pitches to the jury and the partner companies at 13:15, then the jury deliberates while teams give each other feedback. The awards ceremony is at 16:15, and the week ends with White Sensation Night, organised by the students.

??? question "How long is the final pitch?"

    The final pitches follow a 3+3 format.

## For companies { #companies }

??? question "How can my company bring a challenge?"

    Get in touch with the organisers through [match4research.com](https://match4research.com). There is room for a few more challenges this year.

??? question "What makes a good challenge?"

    A good challenge is a real problem your company faces today, open enough that teams can explore different answers and specific enough that they know where to start. Share who is affected, what you have already tried and what a useful result would look like for you. Avoid challenges that already have a fixed solution in mind.

??? question "What is expected from partner companies during the week?"

    Presenting your challenge on Monday morning and taking part in the speed-dating that follows, so teams can ask their questions. Being reachable for follow-up questions during the week helps a lot, and the final pitches on Friday afternoon are where you see the results.

    <span class="ib-tbc">To be confirmed</span> Whether company representatives also sit on the jury.
