# Built on ==good partnerships==

The bootcamp works because people from very different places agree to spend a week at the same table. Companies bring real problems, universities bring students and mentors, and institutions help open doors.
{: .ib-lead }

</div>

</figure>

## Why companies take part { #why-partner }

<div class="ib-moments">
  <div class="ib-moment">
    <span class="ib-moment__when">Fresh eyes</span>
    <strong>New angles on a real problem</strong>
    <span>Several teams work on your challenge for a full week and test their ideas with real people before they pitch.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="ib-moment">
    <span class="ib-moment__when">Talent</span>
    <strong>Meet students before they graduate</strong>
    <span>You see how people think, work in teams and handle pressure, which tells you more than any CV.</span>
  </div>
  <div class="ib-moment">
    <span class="ib-moment__when">Connections</span>
    <strong>A link to universities and mentors</strong>
    <span>Work alongside lecturers and international mentors, and start collaborations that can continue after the week.</span>
  </div>
