## What this module is about

The focus of this module is learning how to use Design Thinking as a human-centred approach to developing innovative solutions to real-life problems. Once a problem has been identified and validated, you need to understand the people affected by it, explore their needs and expectations, and use these insights to develop solutions that create real value.

By working through this module, you will develop a more user-centred and experimental mindset, which is essential when working on real-world innovation challenges.

One common way to picture the process is the double diamond. You first open up to understand the problem and the people who have it, then narrow down to a clear problem. Then you open up again to explore many solutions, and narrow down by testing them.

<figure class="ib-eco ib-eco--compact">
  <img src="../../assets/diagrams/double-diamond.svg" width="720" height="390" alt="A double diamond. In the problem space you understand the user and turn assumptions into insights, arriving at a clear problem. In the solution space you generate many ideas and test them early. What you learn sends you back to improve.">
</figure>

## Questions your team will answer

The bottom line is to move from what you assume to what you have learned. During Tuesday and Wednesday, your team will work through these questions for your challenge.
