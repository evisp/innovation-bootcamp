# Learn at home, ==build on site==

The training helps you think about the impact you can make beyond your academic work. How can project work lead to broader change in society? Which groups could benefit from what you build? The five modules take you from finding a real problem to pitching a solution that makes a difference.
{: .ib-lead }

</div>

<div class="ib-quote">
  <p>The key objective: learning how to make a <mark>real-life impact</mark> with your project and your academic work.</p>
</div>

## How the training works

The training follows a blended learning approach. The knowledge clips cover the concepts, so the onsite workshops can focus on putting them into practice rather than on learning the theory.
{: .ib-lead }

<div class="ib-shift ib-shift--steps">
  <div class="ib-shift__from">
    <span>At home, before each day</span>
    <p>Watch the knowledge clips and read the short materials for the module.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ib-shift__arrow" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  <div class="ib-shift__to">
    <span>On site, with your team</span>
    <p>Apply the concepts <mark>hands-on</mark> to your own challenge, with mentors around you.</p>
  </div>
</div>

Topics such as problem identification, the Sustainable Development Goals and value networks help you bring your project as close as possible to real-life demands, and move you from your comfort zone into the impact-making zone.
