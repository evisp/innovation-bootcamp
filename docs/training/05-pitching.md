## What this module is about

This module is pitch-oriented. It helps you prepare how to present the impact of your project on demo day. Unlike the previous modules, there are no specific questions to answer here, because the pitch is about the project as a whole. Think of it as preparation for the final presentation.

You need to focus on two things at once: your **communication and pitching style**, and the **content of your project**. You will learn how to share your product or service with an audience by explaining your impact, problem, solution and unique selling point in a short time, while keeping their interest high.

## What your pitch needs to get across

In a few minutes, make sure the audience walks away understanding these four things about your project.
{: .ib-lead }

<ol class="ib-questions">
  <li><strong>The problem</strong><span>What it is, who has it, and how you know it is real.</span></li>
  <li><strong>Your solution</strong><span>What you came up with, shown with your prototype if you have one.</span></li>
  <li><strong>Your unique selling point</strong><span>Why your solution and not something else.</span></li>
  <li><strong>Your impact</strong><span>Who it helps, how much, and where else it could make a difference.</span></li>
</ol>
