## What this module is about

This module is centred on one question: how can you make an impact, starting from continuous validation of the problem and of the idea you are working on? Validation pushes you to learn by doing: how to expand your network, and how to create, deliver and capture the value of your project.

It also asks you to think beyond your specific project, and to consider how it could have a broader societal impact by being used in other places where there is a need for it.

<div class="ib-quote">
  <p>If your project is a computer game, could it be adapted for <mark>elderly people with dementia</mark>, or for children who struggle to concentrate?</p>
</div>

<figure class="ib-eco ib-eco--compact">
  <img src="../../assets/diagrams/impact-ripple.svg" width="720" height="400" alt="Concentric circles. Your project is at the centre, surrounded by the people you validated with, then new groups with a similar need, and finally society.">
</figure>

After validating the problem and exploring the wider impact of your project, the next step is learning how to communicate it to the general public and inspire people about your work. That is why this module focuses on customer segments, the unique selling point (USP) and the value proposition.

## Questions your team will answer

This module helps your team answer the following questions about your project.
{: .ib-lead }

<ol class="ib-questions">
  <li><strong>Which pains and gains does your proposed solution address?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>What is the unique selling point of your product, service, prototype or concept?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Which products, services or concepts does your project consist of?</strong><span>It helps to actually visualise your value proposition.</span></li>
  <li><strong>How will you reach your target group?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>How can you turn your project into a long-term, sustainable business model?</strong></li>
</ol>

<div class="ib-callout" markdown>

**Prepare a short slide deck**

Every team prepares a short slide deck answering these questions **before** the progress pitch. There is no time to build it during the session itself, which is for pitching and for giving and receiving feedback.

</div>
