## What this module is about

The focus of this module is learning how to identify a societal need or problem, so that you can turn a technological project into an impactful product or service that adds value and has a positive effect on society. Which capacities do you need to develop alongside your technical skills to spot that need? How do you find it? How do you turn a pain into a gain? Working through these questions helps you build the so-called entrepreneurial mindset, which you will need throughout your professional life.

<div class="ib-quote">
  <p>The idea is not to make you an entrepreneur. It is to help you understand that it is a <mark>societal need that leads the technology</mark>, and not the other way around.</p>
</div>

The workshop concentrates on techniques for discovering a problem, and on how the problem leads you to identify your potential customer, who in turn shapes the solution you come up with. This is how the triangle of customer, problem and solution works.

## Questions your team will answer

The bottom line is to see beyond technology while you are developing technology. By the end of Tuesday, your team should be able to answer these five questions about your challenge.
{: .ib-lead }

<ol class="ib-questions">
  <li><strong>What is the problem you are addressing with your project?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>For whom is this a problem?</strong></li>
  <li><strong>How big is the problem?</strong><span>How can you quantify its size, so that you can also quantify your impact?</span></li>
  <li><strong>How can you validate that this is actually a problem?</strong><span>Which techniques will you use?</span></li>
  <li><strong>What does your validation network look like?</strong><span>And how can you expand it?</span></li>
</ol>
