## Every team goes through stages

Most teams move through four stages: forming, storming, norming and performing. The Tuesday session starts here. The most useful thing to know in advance is that the dip in the middle is normal. When ideas start clashing, it usually means the team is getting somewhere.
{: .ib-lead }

### Six Thinking Hats

A simple method for team discussions: everyone looks at the problem from the same angle at the same time, one hat at a time, instead of arguing from different angles at once.
{: .ib-lead }

<div class="ib-hats">
  <div class="ib-hat ib-hat--white"><span></span><strong>White</strong><small>Facts and data. What do we know?</small></div>
  <div class="ib-hat ib-hat--red"><span></span><strong>Red</strong><small>Feelings and gut reactions.</small></div>
  <div class="ib-hat ib-hat--black"><span></span><strong>Black</strong><small>Risks. What could go wrong?</small></div>
  <div class="ib-hat ib-hat--yellow"><span></span><strong>Yellow</strong><small>Benefits. What could go right?</small></div>
  <div class="ib-hat ib-hat--green"><span></span><strong>Green</strong><small>New ideas and alternatives.</small></div>
  <div class="ib-hat ib-hat--blue"><span></span><strong>Blue</strong><small>The process. What do we do next?</small></div>
</div>

## Worth agreeing on as a team

A few minutes on these questions early in the week saves a lot of time later.
{: .ib-lead }

<ol class="ib-questions">
  <li><strong>Who is good at what?</strong><span>Know each other's strengths before you split the work.</span></li>
  <li><strong>How will we make decisions?</strong><span>By agreement, by vote, or does someone have the final say on certain topics?</span></li>
  <li><strong>What do we do when we disagree?</strong><span>Try the six hats before it turns into a debate.</span></li>
</ol>
