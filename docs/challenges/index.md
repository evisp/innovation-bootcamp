## What the jury looks for

On Friday the jury evaluates every pitch on the same five criteria, whichever challenge your team chose.
{: .ib-lead }

<ol class="ib-criteria">
  <li><strong>Innovation</strong><span>Is the idea new, or a fresh take on the problem?</span></li>
  <li><strong>Relevance</strong><span>Does it answer the company's question and the need behind it?</span></li>
  <li><strong>Feasibility</strong><span>Could it realistically be built and put to use?</span></li>
  <li><strong>Scalability</strong><span>Could it grow beyond a first version or a first group of users?</span></li>
  <li><strong>Potential impact</strong><span>How much difference could it make, and for whom?</span></li>
</ol>
